# Sales-and-Customer-Insights-Dashboard
"A dynamic Power bi dashboard analyzing $24.9M in total sales and customer demographics. Includes MTD/YTD tracking, profit margin analysis, and customer segmentation by education and gender."

# Financial Overview: 
Tracking Total Sales ($24.91M), Total Profit ($10.46M), and a healthy 54% AVG Profit Margin.

# Time-Series Analysis: 
Monthly sales growth from September to June and performance by weekday (Saturday peaks).

# Customer Demographics: 
Analysis of 18K customers, segmented by gender (near 50/50 split) and education level (Bachelors being the largest group).

# Top Performers: 
Visualizing the Top 10 customers by sales volume.
